About

&& is a global interior design firm and spatial design practice with expertise across Copenhagen, New York, Tokyo, and Seoul. We operate across commercial interior design, architecture, and installations, specializing in creating transformative office interiors, retail spaces, restaurants, and hospitality environments in major cities worldwide. We create meaningful spatial experiences that translate a brand's identity and seasonal narratives into immersive environments. At our core we create meaningful connections between people and brands. From early concept through delivery, we protect this core purpose, turning complex briefs into enduring, context-led spaces.
